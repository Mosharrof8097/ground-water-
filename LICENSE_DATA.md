# Data licence and required acknowledgement

The CSV files under `00_data/raw/` are **not** the work of this project. They
are the public release of the National Hydrochemical Survey of Bangladesh and
are redistributed here unchanged so that the analysis can be reproduced.

Reuse is free, but any publication using these data must cite the source and
acknowledge the funder and partners:

> BGS and DPHE (2001). *Arsenic contamination of groundwater in Bangladesh.*
> Kinniburgh, D.G. and Smedley, P.L. (eds). British Geological Survey Technical
> Report WC/00/19. British Geological Survey, Keyworth.

The survey was funded by DFID and carried out by the British Geological Survey
with the Department of Public Health Engineering, Government of Bangladesh.

File integrity can be checked with `md5sum -c 00_data/raw/CHECKSUMS.md5`.
Full provenance, including source URLs and download date, is in
`00_data/README.md`.
