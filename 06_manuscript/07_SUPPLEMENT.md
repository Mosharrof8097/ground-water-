# Supplementary material

Supporting tables and methodological detail for *Surrogate screening of trace
metals in Bangladesh groundwater*. Every table is generated by the analysis
code and read from disk; none is transcribed by hand. The pipeline that
produces them is at https://github.com/Mosharrof8097/ground-water-.

---

## S1. Censoring read two ways

The paper argues that a detection limit read as though it were a measurement
can manufacture structure as readily as it can hide it. Both cases below come
from this analysis and are reported in full so the claim can be checked.

{{TABLE:S_CENSORING_CASES}}

In the first case the test is nitrate at or above 0.5 mg/L. Nitrate is censored
in 80 % of these wells at limits of 0.3 and 0.5 mg/L, so a well reported as
"< 0.5" carries the value 0.5 and passes. In the second the target is modelled
on a log scale, where the lower tail carries disproportionate weight and the
split between the two arsenic detection limits of 0.5 and 6.0 µg/L is itself
learnable. Neither survives when the censoring flag is respected.
